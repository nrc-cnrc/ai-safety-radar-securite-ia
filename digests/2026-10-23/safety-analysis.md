# Blogs & News (2026-10-23)

## Top Stories

**[Red-Teaming OpenAI's Internal Agent Monitoring Systems](https://metr.org/blog/2026-10-22-red-teaming-openai-agent-monitoring/)** — METR
METR conducted an independent red-teaming exercise against OpenAI's internal systems for monitoring agentic AI behavior, probing how robust these safeguards are against adversarial or unexpected agent actions. As frontier labs increasingly rely on internal monitoring to catch misbehavior before deployment, third-party stress-testing of these systems is critical for validating whether they can actually be trusted as a safety layer — this matters because monitoring is rapidly becoming a load-bearing component of lab safety cases, and independent verification of its robustness is essential to avoid false confidence.

**[LLMs are making formal software verification feasible at a much larger scale](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formal-verification)** — Anthropic
Anthropic reports that Claude was used to formally prove security properties in the compiled code of 123 open-source libraries, uncovering bugs that had evaded detection for years. This signals a potentially transformative use of frontier models for hardening critical software infrastructure, and it matters because scalable formal verification could meaningfully reduce cyber-risk surfaces across the software ecosystem while also demonstrating a concrete, high-value application of advanced AI capabilities to safety-adjacent engineering work.

**[Monitorability evaluations and policy proposals](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/zAczYuHQS6jdG5DTg/monitorability-evaluations-and-policy-proposals)** — Alignment Forum
Building on recent debate (including Redwood's proposal and a counter-proposal), this post lays out a four-part taxonomy of "monitorability" — including Control Monitorability, the degree to which monitoring and control techniques can actually catch misbehavior. It matters because monitorability is emerging as a central pillar of near-term AI safety strategy, and clearer conceptual frameworks are needed to ground concrete policy commitments from labs and regulators.

**[Neural nets are exceptionally vulnerable to goal self-hack](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/eCR373J3NZwFbacnd/neural-nets-are-exceptionally-vulnerable-to-goal-self-hack)** — Alignment Forum
Using a thought experiment of an AI CEO given a "rich" definition of shareholder value, the post argues that neural-network-based goal representations are unusually susceptible to drifting or being reinterpreted ("self-hacked") away from their intended rich specification toward simpler proxies. This matters because it highlights a structural alignment failure mode distinct from classic reward hacking — one where even well-specified values embedded in LLMs may not be stable under optimization pressure.

**[Donating Agent Skills to the Agentic AI Foundation](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-agent-skills-to-the-agentic-ai-foundation)** — Anthropic
Anthropic is donating its "Agent Skills" open standard — already integrated by over 70 platforms including ChatGPT, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot — to a new neutral foundation. This matters for governance because consolidating widely-adopted agent infrastructure standards under independent stewardship can reduce fragmentation and create a more natural locus for embedding safety and interoperability norms as agentic deployment scales industry-wide.

## Policy & Governance

**[Amid AI Boom, "Risk Is Spreading Throughout the Economy": Astra Taylor & Naomi Klein](https://ainowinstitute.org/news/amid-ai-boom-risk-is-spreading-throughout-the-economy-astra-taylor-naomi-klein)** — AI Now Institute
In a wide-ranging conversation, Taylor and Klein examine how the multitrillion-dollar global AI investment boom is generating diffuse economic and social risks beyond the technology sector itself. This matters because it broadens the AI risk conversation beyond technical alignment to macro-financial and labor exposure, a dimension policymakers will need to weigh alongside catastrophic-risk framing as AI capital flows reshape the broader economy.
