# Blogs & News (2026-11-11)

## Top Stories

**1. METR's Acceleration Report Ignites Intelligence Explosion Debate**
A new [METR assessment](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/ai-r-and-d-is-accelerating-towards) finding that Anthropic is improving model capabilities on well-specified tasks at roughly 2x its historical rate (plausibly 1.3x–3x, with ~80% attributed to AI contributions) has triggered a wave of analysis. [Zvi Mowshowitz's deep dive](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/ai-r-and-d-is-accelerating-towards) and the [Alignment Forum's Q3.5 Timelines Update](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/f9PajMLmBYzmWzyDv/q3-5-timelines-update-looks-like-the-intelligence-explosion) both extrapolate from this data toward a potential intelligence explosion as early as 2027, while a more skeptical [LessWrong rebuttal](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/7znBtiPuQybFLxjmj/do-verifiable-metrics-overestimate-rsi) questions whether verifiable-metric gains overstate true recursive self-improvement (RSI) potential. Dean Ball's [Hyperdimensional essay](https://www.hyperdimensional.co/p/iron-makes-iron) adds a conceptual distinction between two different mechanisms people lump together as "RSI." *This matters because if the acceleration estimates hold up, timelines for transformative AI—and the urgency of safety work—could compress dramatically, making this the single most consequential empirical signal of the week.*

**2. Google DeepMind May Be Training on Chain-of-Thought, Undermining Monitorability**
A [LessWrong investigation](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/JRYKFspYzCo97GqPN/we-don-t-understand-whether-gdm-intentionally-trains-on-cot) flags language in the Gemini 4 safety report suggesting GDM's RL training observes and up-weights reasoning within the chain-of-thought itself, creating optimization pressure on individual CoT tokens. This runs against community norms (per Kaufmann et al. 2026) that reward signals should avoid conflicting with faithful reasoning traces. *This matters because CoT faithfulness is one of the few current tools for interpreting model intentions, and even unintentional training pressure on CoT content could quietly erode this safety-relevant transparency mechanism.*

**3. A Sharper Taxonomy for "Automating Alignment"**
An [Alignment Forum post](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/NFkj9J2BBLSjNuH5G/how-i-m-thinking-about-different-types-of-automated) proposes splitting "automated alignment research" into empirical, conceptual, and (implied) other categories, arguing the strategic implications differ substantially by type. *This matters because as labs race to automate alignment research itself, conflating these categories could lead to overconfidence—automating empirical experiment-running is not the same as automating the conceptual judgment calls that determine what "aligned" even means.*

**4. Proposal: Mandatory Minimum Testing Windows Before Internal Deployment**
A new [Alignment Forum proposal](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/XNLhpWgzhF8GYZ4Er/delaying-internal-deployments-until-after-x-days-of-safety-testing) suggests labs commit to a fixed minimum safety-testing period before any new model—even internally—can be deployed, with tunable parameters for duration, scope, and trigger point. *This matters because internal deployment (e.g., using new models to accelerate R&D, as in the METR findings above) is increasingly where frontier capability gains compound fastest, yet it typically receives far less external scrutiny than public releases.*

## Policy & Governance

**[AISN #85: US Midterms Favor Candidates Backing AI Regulation](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/aisn-85-us-midterms-favor-candidates)** — CAIS's newsletter reports that midterm election outcomes favored candidates supportive of AI regulation, alongside continued strategic disagreement among AI safety advocates about tactics. *This matters because electoral signals shape the near-term feasibility of federal AI legislation, which safety advocates have struggled to advance.*

**[The U.S.–China AI Competition: Two Paths to AGI](https://cset.georgetown.edu/article/the-u-s-china-ai-competition-two-paths-to-artificial-general-intelligence/)** (Georgetown CSET) — A forthcoming study argues China is pursuing a broader AGI strategy encompassing robotics, embodiment, and brain-inspired computing, rather than just matching the US on frontier LLM scale. *This matters because US policy framed around a single-axis "compute race" may miss key vectors of Chinese AI progress, with implications for export controls and competitiveness strategy.*

**[Transparency Disclosures for Chain-of-Thought Monitorability](https://www.iaps.ai/research/transparency-disclosures-for-chain-of-thought-monitorability)** (IAPS) — This report proposes disclosure standards for how labs handle CoT monitorability, directly relevant to the GDM training-on-CoT concerns raised above. *This matters because standardized transparency disclosures could let outside researchers verify claims about CoT faithfulness rather than relying on ambiguous safety-report language.*

**[Palantir Hires Former Democratic Senator Amid Congressional Scrutiny](https://ainowinstitute.org/news/palantir-hires-a-former-democratic-senator-as-it-faces-scrutiny-in-new-congress)** (AI Now Institute) — AI Now's Aya Ibrahim warns this is part of a broader pattern of tech firms using political hires to blunt accountability efforts. *This matters because it illustrates how government-AI contractors are proactively managing political risk as congressional oversight of AI/surveillance firms intensifies.*
