# Daily Digest (2026-11-11)

## Top 3 Developments

**Multilateral Coalition Publishes Second Major Governance Framework in Two Days** - [Advancing a Global Framework for AI Safety and Governance for the Well-being of Humanity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13968v1), authored by dozens of researchers and policy figures including Seán Ó hÉigeartaigh and Wendy Hall under the World Internet Conference's AI Safety and Governance Program, draws lessons from nuclear and climate multilateralism to propose a human-wellbeing-centered governance framework (ongoing theme from yesterday's companion report, [Frontier AI Safety and Governance 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12434v1)). This matters because the rapid succession of these reports signals active convergence on shared governance vocabulary between Chinese and international stakeholders ahead of any binding rules.

**IAPS Proposes Chain-of-Thought Transparency Disclosures as GDM Faces Scrutiny Over Training on CoT** - [Transparency Disclosures for Chain-of-Thought Monitorability](https://www.iaps.ai/research/transparency-disclosures-for-chain-of-thought-monitorability) proposes standards for labs to disclose how they handle CoT monitorability, landing the same day a [LessWrong investigation](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/JRYKFspYzCo97GqPN/we-don-t-understand-whether-gdm-intentionally-trains-on-cot) flags language in Google DeepMind's Gemini 4 safety report suggesting RL training may be up-weighting reasoning within chain-of-thought itself. This matters because CoT faithfulness is one of the few current tools for inferring model intent, and ambiguity over whether training pressure corrupts it strengthens the case for the standardized disclosures IAPS proposes.

**Systematization of Knowledge Maps the Expanding Attack Surface of Agentic AI** - [Agents of Chaos: A SoK on Malicious Attacks on Agentic AI Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10843v1) consolidates fragmented prior findings into a unified taxonomy of how adversaries exploit tools, memory, retrieval, and multi-agent coordination to hijack planning and tool use. This matters because a shared taxonomy is a prerequisite for prioritizing defenses as agentic deployments scale faster than security practice.

## Risk Watch

**Increased Risks**
- A wave of HIGH-severity command-injection CVEs across MCP servers, triaged via [agent-airlock](https://github.com/sattyamjjain/agent-airlock), shows agent tool ecosystems shipping shell-execution capability faster than they secure it.
- [NVIDIA-NeMo Guardrails](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Guardrails/pull/2257) and [PromptKit](https://github.com/AltairaLabs/PromptKit/issues/2660) both had fail-open defaults in supposedly best-practice guardrail/HITL components, undermining their core safety guarantee.
- A [LessWrong investigation](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/JRYKFspYzCo97GqPN/we-don-t-understand-whether-gdm-intentionally-trains-on-cot) suggests Google DeepMind's RL training may be optimizing chain-of-thought tokens directly, risking erosion of CoT faithfulness as a monitoring tool.
- [From Attack Surface to Defense Architecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13960v1) argues coding agents with shell/network access function as automated "Living-off-the-Land" platforms once prompt injection occurs, a structural vulnerability current blacklist defenses don't address.

**Decreased Risks**
- [IAPS's proposal](https://www.iaps.ai/research/transparency-disclosures-for-chain-of-thought-monitorability) for standardized CoT monitorability disclosures offers a concrete mechanism to verify lab claims rather than relying on ambiguous safety-report language.
- [pytest-wardenbot's v0.5.0 release](https://github.com/wardenbot-ai/pytest-wardenbot/releases/tag/v0.5.0) publicly rebuilt its leak-detection grading after finding it too lenient, a rare transparent fix to measurement validity in agent-eval tooling.
- [agent-airlock v0.10.33](https://github.com/sattyamjjain/agent-airlock/releases/tag/v0.10.33) replaced marketing-style "Full" MCP coverage claims with auditable, evidence-backed labels.
- [Agents of Chaos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10843v1) consolidates fragmented attack-vector knowledge into a shared taxonomy that should improve prioritization of agentic AI defenses.
