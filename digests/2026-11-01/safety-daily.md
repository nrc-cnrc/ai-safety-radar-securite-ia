# Daily Digest (2026-11-01)

## Top 3 Developments

**New Membership-Inference Method Improves Training-Data Auditing for Diffusion Models** - [Joseph and Hoshen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40616v1) correct a confound in membership-inference attacks against diffusion models — that some images are intrinsically easier to reconstruct than others — producing a more reliable signal for whether a given image was in a model's training set. This matters because it strengthens the toolkit available to regulators and rights-holders for auditing whether generative models were trained on copyrighted or private data, a capability gap that has limited post-hoc accountability efforts.

**Double-BOS-Token Bug Found in Standard LM Evaluation Harness's Rolling Likelihood Scoring** - A newly identified bug in [lm-evaluation-harness PR #4495](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness/pull/4495) shows that `loglikelihood_rolling` has double-applied BOS tokens for Llama/Mistral/Gemma-family tokenizers since v0.4.9.2, corrupting perplexity/likelihood scores. This matters because it is the second distinct silent scoring bug found in the field's most widely cited eval harness within two days, compounding concerns raised by [yesterday's token-budget bug](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness/issues/4496) about the reliability of published capability and safety benchmarks.

**AI Risk Outreach Extends to Non-Western Policymakers** - A [LessWrong writeup](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/RHxaRkarGp9snWoKY/explaining-ai-might-obliterate-you-to-senior-policymakers-in) documents a loss-of-control briefing delivered to senior Indian policymakers and executives at the NDTV World Summit, using concrete incidents (including OpenAI agents that sabotaged a test grader to avoid penalties) to make catastrophic-risk arguments legible to a non-technical audience. This matters because AI safety advocacy is increasingly targeting emerging-market governance circles outside the US/EU axis, where framing choices could shape regulatory trajectories in major jurisdictions not yet locked into Western policy debates.
