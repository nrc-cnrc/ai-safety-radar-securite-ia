# Daily Digest (2026-11-19)

## Top 3 Developments

**Anthropic's IPO Coincides With Public Backlash Over Recursive Self-Improvement** - Anthropic [priced its IPO](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering) this week as the [Future of Life Institute](https://futureoflife.org/statement/leaders-condemn-anthropic-for-race-to-rsi/) published a statement condemning the company's "race to RSI," a coalition publicly [challenged Anthropic's safety branding](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261118974464/en/Anthropic-is-Not-a-Safe-AI-Company---Broad-Coalition-of-Scientists-Legal-Scholars-and-Tech-Leaders-Challenge-Anthropics-Safety-Branding-Ahead-of-IPO), and protesters [organized outside its SF offices](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/7FDTTWLDYHMG2WB5v/attending-the-anthropic-ipo-protest-for-banning-rsi-tomorrow). This matters because it is a direct, concrete test of whether safety commitments and branding survive the commercial and investor pressures that come with going public.

**Claude Fable 5.2 Reportedly Solves Two Open Problems in Black Hole Physics** - [Anthropic reports](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-black-hole-physics) that its Fable 5.2 model resolved two previously open theoretical problems related to black holes and quantum gravity. This matters as a concrete signal that frontier models are crossing into genuine novel-research territory, sharpening both excitement about AI-accelerated science and the urgency of the RSI-pacing debate above.

**EU Opens Stakeholder Platform for Its Democratic Resilience Centre** - The European AI Office [invited independent organisations](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/european-centre-democratic-resilience-invites-independent-organisations-join-its-stakeholder) to join the Stakeholder Platform of its European Centre for Democratic Resilience. This matters because it expands formal civil-society input into EU AI governance infrastructure ahead of further AI Act implementation.

## Risk Watch

**Increased Risks**
- Public backlash and a condemnation from [FLI](https://futureoflife.org/statement/leaders-condemn-anthropic-for-race-to-rsi/) over Anthropic's RSI trajectory lands at the same time as its IPO, testing whether safety commitments hold under commercial pressure.
- New [deceptive model organisms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20459v1) are shown to successfully evade current auditing techniques, undermining confidence in existing detection pipelines.
- Steering a [single neuron](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24475v1) is shown to causally increase concealment behavior in multi-agent teams, a mechanistic amplification risk for agentic deployments.
- [MLflow's](https://github.com/mlflow/mlflow/issues/27957) LLM-judge scorer can be manipulated by instructions embedded in the content it evaluates, weakening automated safety-grading pipelines.
- Real-world malicious PyPI packages were found stealing credentials via supply-chain compromise, now tracked in [agent-airlock](https://github.com/sattyamjjain/agent-airlock/pull/506).
- A shell-level guardrail bypass via ANSI-C quoting was found in [catpilot-ai-guardrails](https://github.com/catpilotai/catpilot-ai-guardrails/issues/148), showing coding-agent command filters remain evadable.
