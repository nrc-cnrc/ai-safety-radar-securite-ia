# Blogs & News (2026-11-19)

## Top Stories

**Anthropic's IPO Lands Amid Growing Backlash Over Recursive Self-Improvement**
Anthropic [priced its initial public offering](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering) this week, a major milestone that coincided with mounting public criticism of the company's AI development trajectory. The [Future of Life Institute published a statement](https://futureoflife.org/statement/leaders-condemn-anthropic-for-race-to-rsi/) from leading figures condemning Anthropic for what they characterize as a "reckless race" toward recursive self-improvement (RSI), arguing that AI systems capable of improving themselves could rapidly escalate risk beyond manageable levels. The criticism has translated into direct action: a [planned protest outside Anthropic's San Francisco offices](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/7FDTTWLDYHMG2WB5v/attending-the-anthropic-ipo-protest-for-banning-rsi-tomorrow) (via [banrsi.ai](https://banrsi.ai/)) calls for government bans on RSI or voluntary halts by labs, with organizers arguing that even safety-focused labs like Anthropic are overconfident about their ability to control self-improving systems. Separately, [Dean Ball's "The Silent Consensus"](https://www.hyperdimensional.co/p/the-silent-consensus) pushes back against this framing, arguing against what he sees as premature capability "forfeiture." This matters because it marks a visible rupture between the safety-advocacy community and a leading "safety-first" lab, right as that lab enters public markets — raising hard questions about whether commercial pressure and safety commitments can coexist as RSI capabilities advance.

**Claude Fable 5.2 Solves Two Open Problems in Black Hole Physics**
Anthropic announced that its [Claude Fable 5.2 model solved two previously open theoretical physics problems](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-black-hole-physics) related to black holes and the search for a theory of quantum gravity. This matters because autonomous scientific discovery at this level is a concrete capability signal that frontier models are crossing into genuine novel research territory — intensifying both excitement about AI-accelerated science and concerns (echoed in the RSI debate above) about how fast capabilities are advancing relative to our ability to understand and control them.

**METR Report Triggers Timeline Reassessments**
Zvi Mowshowitz's ["Quickly, There's No Time"](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/quickly-theres-no-time) discusses how a recent METR report has prompted widespread recalibration of AI timelines across the community, with many observers moving toward shorter expected horizons for transformative capabilities. This matters because shifts in expert timeline estimates directly affect prioritization of safety research, governance urgency, and risk communication — and a credible empirical source (METR) moving the needle carries more weight than speculative debate.

**A Concrete Proposal for Pacing Frontier AI Development**
A new proposal, [Pacing AI via Compute Reallocation](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/dBEDh2WeZfhuiDnJ5/pacing-ai-via-compute-reallocation) (hosted at [pacing-ai.com](https://pacing-ai.com)), lays out a two-part governance mechanism: (1) Verified Compute Allocation Disclosures (VCAD), requiring AI companies to disclose compute usage with independent auditor verification, and (2) mandated reallocation of compute away from capabilities R&D. This matters because it offers one of the more concrete, verifiable technical governance levers for slowing frontier capability growth — directly relevant to the RSI-pacing debate dominating this week's discourse.

**Technical Alignment Progress: Reflective Oracle Solomonoff Induction**
A [PIBBSS fellow's paper on reflective-oracle Solomonoff induction (rOSI)](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/nvrFYAwciyyj9T5ir/solomonoff-induction-with-reflective-oracles), linked from the Alignment Forum, extends reflective oracles — previously known mainly for game-theoretic use cases — into a new sequence-prediction framework with favorable theoretical properties. This matters because foundational decision-theory and prediction-theory work underpins longer-term alignment research agendas, even if its near-term practical impact is limited.

## Policy & Governance

**EU Launches Stakeholder Platform for Democratic Resilience Centre**
The European AI Office announced that its [European Centre for Democratic Resilience is inviting independent organisations to join its Stakeholder Platform](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/european-centre-democratic-resilience-invites-independent-organisations-join-its-stakeholder). This matters because it signals the EU's continued build-out of institutional infrastructure to monitor AI's societal and democratic impacts, broadening the set of civil-society voices with formal input into EU digital governance ahead of further AI Act implementation.
