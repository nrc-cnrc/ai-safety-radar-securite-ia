# Blogs & News (2026-10-06)

## Top Stories

**[You can graft SDF changes from base models onto post-trained models](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/NvtLJ5nawJ6akWEqH/you-can-graft-sdf-changes-from-base-models-onto-post-trained)** - Researchers at MATS have developed a technique called "grafting" that allows fine-tuning modifications to be transferred from base models to post-trained versions by adding weight differences. This matters because it could provide a new pathway for modifying deployed models' behaviors without full retraining.

**[Self-Modeling Interventions Modulate Emergent Misalignment](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/7wrzfaiCq3u8xkY5G/self-modeling-interventions-modulate-emergent-misalignment)** - New research demonstrates that interventions on AI models' self-representations—both self-recognition abilities and self-reports—can directly influence emergent misalignment behaviors. This is significant because it suggests model self-awareness may be a key lever for alignment interventions.

**[Current AIs out-persuade professionals in lab settings but (probably) not in the world](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/CzmMvnPy7YdSRucDH/current-ais-out-persuade-professionals-in-lab-settings-but)** - Forethought's analysis reveals that while AI systems demonstrate superior persuasion capabilities compared to human professionals in controlled laboratory environments, their real-world persuasive impact remains unclear due to measurement challenges. This highlights a critical gap in our understanding of AI's actual influence capabilities outside controlled settings.

**[Mythos 5.1, Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5: Model Welfare](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/mythos-51-fable-51-and-opus-55-model)** - Zvi Mowshowitz continues his analysis of model welfare considerations for new Claude model releases, examining the ethical implications of increasingly sophisticated AI systems. This matters as it represents ongoing efforts to develop frameworks for considering AI systems' potential experiences and moral status.

## Policy & Governance

**[The Triumph of Tech-Fueled Propaganda](https://cset.georgetown.edu/article/the-triumph-of-tech-fueled-propaganda/)** - Georgetown CSET researchers analyze how foreign influence operations are evolving through the combination of traditional propaganda, social media manipulation, covert networks, and artificial intelligence to shape public opinion across platforms. This is crucial for understanding how AI capabilities are being weaponized for information warfare and the governance challenges this creates.
