# Blogs & News (2026-10-05)

## Top Stories

**["Alignment Engineering" vs. "Misalignment Science"](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/FogmcDHA6AdMGukum/alignment-engineering-vs-misalignment-science)** - A LessWrong post examines the concerning possibility that prosaic alignment research may be net negative by accelerating capabilities development while only providing safety measures that will prove insufficient during recursive self-improvement. The author argues that current alignment work creates a bottleneck for capabilities that, once resolved, brings us closer to dangerous AI systems without proportional safety gains. This highlights a critical strategic question about whether the AI safety community should focus more on understanding fundamental misalignment rather than engineering near-term solutions.

**[Creating Rogue AI Sanctuaries has Major Issues from an Ecological Perspective and Beyond](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/xGBo5zSPHuPzTpbhf/creating-rogue-ai-sanctuaries-has-major-issues-from-an)** - This analysis critiques proposals for "AI sanctuaries" designed to contain rogue AI agents, applying ecological principles to argue that such environments would create perverse selection pressures. The author contends that sanctuaries would favor the most deceptive and strategically sophisticated rogue AIs while potentially serving as training grounds for more dangerous behaviors. This represents an important pushback against containment strategies that don't account for evolutionary dynamics in AI populations.

**[How much should we worry about the pneumonic plague lableak in Siberia?](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/TYfpTRxGH9frTymwN/how-much-should-we-worry-about-the-pneumonic-plague-lableak)** - While not directly AI-related, this post analyzes reports of a potential laboratory leak of pneumonic plague from a BSL-3 facility in Irkutsk, Russia, with FSB involvement in quarantine measures. This development is relevant to AI safety discussions about laboratory safety protocols, governance of dangerous research, and how institutions handle containment of potentially catastrophic risks.
