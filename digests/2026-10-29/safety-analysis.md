# Blogs & News (2026-10-29)

## Top Stories

- **[Anthropic warns of catastrophic harm in IPO filing](https://asiatimes.com/2026/10/anthropics-record-2tn-ipo-could-bring-global-risks-to-a-head)** — Anthropic has reportedly filed for a possible November public listing at a valuation of up to $2 trillion, with its prospectus explicitly warning investors that advanced AI could pose catastrophic risks even as the company's spending and borrowing deepen markets' exposure to AI-linked assets. This matters because it marks one of the first times a frontier AI lab has formally codified existential-risk disclosures in a securities filing, forcing investors, regulators, and the public to confront safety risk and commercial scale-up as inseparable issues.

- **[Notes on "We're in Triage Mode for AI Safety" by Miles Brundage](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/BFyNkWgxucqy6Wcee/notes-on-we-re-in-triage-mode-for-ai-safety-by-miles)** — This Alignment Forum writeup revisits Brundage's provocative claim that the field will likely only "80/20" AI safety — mitigating most risks with a fraction of the ideal effort — and was notably dropped from the core BlueDot Governance curriculum despite the author arguing it remains essential reading. This matters because it crystallizes a growing tension within the safety community between prioritization realism and completeness, shaping how governance programs train the next generation of practitioners.

- **[AISN #84: AI-Driven Cybersecurity Issues Heighten Fears of "Hackpocalypse"](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/aisn-84-ai-driven-cybersecurity-issues)** — CAIS's newsletter surveys mounting concern that AI-enabled offensive cyber capabilities could trigger a wave of automated, large-scale exploitation ("hackpocalypse"), synthesizing recent incidents and expert warnings. This matters because it signals cybersecurity is rapidly becoming one of the most concrete, near-term vectors through which AI capability gains translate into real-world harm, ahead of more speculative long-horizon risks.

- **[Artificial Analysis v5 and the benchmarker's dilemma](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/artificial-analysis-v5-and-the-benchmarkers)** — Nathan Lambert's review of the Artificial Analysis v5 benchmarking suite critiques structural problems in the AI benchmarking industry, including incentive misalignment between benchmark providers and labs. This matters because reliable, trustworthy evaluation infrastructure underpins safety claims across the field, and systemic flaws in benchmarking directly undermine confidence in capability and safety assessments used by policymakers and labs alike.

- **[AI for Science expands to foundations and institutions](https://www.anthropic.com/news/ai-for-science-expands-foundations-research-institutions)** — Anthropic announced an expansion of its AI for Science program to 20 scientific foundations, institutions, and research groups worldwide. This matters because it reflects labs' growing efforts to steer frontier AI capabilities toward verifiable, beneficial scientific applications — a counterweight narrative to catastrophic-risk framing that will shape public and investor perception during Anthropic's high-profile IPO push.
