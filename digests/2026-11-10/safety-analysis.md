# Blogs & News (2026-11-10)

## Top Stories

**[Ajeya: AI is dramatically accelerating AI research right now](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/8tL9f5HZxsTvn2LHp/ajeya-ai-is-dramatically-accelerating-ai-research-right-now)** — Anthropic's recent S-1 filing disclosed that the company "may imminently meet" the threshold defined in its Responsible Scaling Policy for "dramatically accelerated progress due to AI" — specifically, overall capabilities progress running twice as fast as the fastest pre-AI sustained rate. This builds on a new [METR report](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/ai-r-and-d-is-accelerating-towards) estimating a 1.7x acceleration in Anthropic's capability progress from AI-assisted R&D, which commentators argue implies research-task-level speedups of 10x or more. **Why it matters**: If a leading lab is approaching its own self-declared recursive self-improvement trigger, this is a critical real-world test of whether RSP commitments translate into actual safeguards once the threshold is crossed — and a bellwether for how fast the overall AI R&D feedback loop may be tightening industry-wide.

**[Where is all the AI safety research uplift?](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/wPrpnfaNL5JP9i6KT/where-is-all-the-ai-safety-research-uplift)** — A pointed Alignment Forum critique questions why, if AI is providing 10x+ uplift to capabilities research (per Ajeya's and Anthropic staff's estimates), there isn't a commensurate visible surge in alignment and safety research output. **Why it matters**: This asymmetry — capabilities uplift outpacing safety uplift — is a central concern for whether labs' internal safety work can keep pace with self-accelerating capabilities progress, and is likely to shape near-term debates about resource allocation inside frontier labs.

**[AI R&D Is Accelerating Towards Potential RSI in 2027](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/ai-r-and-d-is-accelerating-towards)** — Zvi Mowshowitz's deep dive into the METR report, unpacking what Anthropic's RSP threshold-crossing would mean in practice, how reliable the 1.7x estimate is, and trajectories toward recursive self-improvement as early as 2027. **Why it matters**: Zvi's synthesis is becoming a key reference point for how the wider safety community interprets lab-reported acceleration metrics, which are otherwise opaque and hard to independently verify.

**[Anthropic launches roadshow for proposed initial public offering](https://www.anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch)** — Anthropic formally began its IPO roadshow for Class A common stock. **Why it matters**: Going public will subject Anthropic's safety commitments (including its RSP) to new shareholder and market pressures, raising questions about whether commercial incentives from public markets could conflict with safety-driven deployment decisions at a moment when the company itself says it may be nearing an RSI-relevant threshold.

**[Claude can design electronic prototypes from start to finish](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-electronics-design)** — Anthropic reports that Claude successfully designed three working electronic devices (a speakerphone, voice-assistant hub, and handheld virtual pet) with no circuitry fixes required. **Why it matters**: This is a concrete capability datapoint for AI moving into end-to-end physical/hardware engineering, relevant to assessing how far automation is extending beyond software into domains with real-world safety and dual-use implications.

## Policy & Governance

**[The U.S.–China AI Competition: Two Paths to Artificial General Intelligence](https://cset.georgetown.edu/article/the-u-s-china-ai-competition-two-paths-to-artificial-general-intelligence/)** — A forthcoming Georgetown CSET study argues that while the U.S. leads in frontier models and compute, China is pursuing a broader, more diversified path to AGI via robotics, embodiment, and brain-inspired architectures. **Why it matters**: This challenges the dominant "single race" framing in U.S. policy circles, suggesting that export controls and compute-focused strategies may miss key vectors of Chinese AI progress.

**[Palantir hires a former Democratic senator as it faces scrutiny in new Congress](https://ainowinstitute.org/news/palantir-hires-a-former-democratic-senator-as-it-faces-scrutiny-in-new-congress)** — AI Now Institute highlights Palantir's hiring of a former senator amid incoming congressional scrutiny, framing it as part of a broader pattern of tech companies using reputation management and lobbying to preempt accountability. **Why it matters**: This signals how AI/data companies with deep government ties are positioning themselves ahead of anticipated regulatory and oversight battles in the new Congress.

**[Lightcone Commons: Round 1 complete, Round 2 underway](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/3ASLq8ePNqkbeGqdj/lightcone-commons-round-1-complete-round-2-underway)** — A new coordinated funding mechanism for AI safety and adjacent projects closed its first round, with 23 funders granting $45.1M across 147 projects (evaluated by figures including Zvi Mowshowitz and Eliezer Yudkowsky); monthly rounds are planned for 2027. **Why it matters**: This represents a significant, fast-growing piece of independent funding infrastructure for AI safety work outside major labs, potentially reshaping how the field allocates capital to non-lab safety research.
