# Daily Digest (2026-11-10)

## Top 3 Developments

**Multi-Stakeholder Coalition Publishes Comprehensive China-International Frontier AI Governance Roadmap** - [Frontier AI Safety and Governance 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12434v1), authored by a large international coalition including Seán Ó hÉigeartaigh and Yaodong Yang under the World Internet Conference's AI Safety and Governance Program, systematically maps the frontier-risk landscape, ranks response priorities, and reviews existing global governance mechanisms. This matters because it represents one of the most comprehensive attempts to align Chinese and international stakeholders on a shared risk taxonomy, a prerequisite for any workable coordinated oversight regime.

**CSET Study Challenges "Single Race" Framing of US-China AI Competition** - [The U.S.–China AI Competition: Two Paths to Artificial General Intelligence](https://cset.georgetown.edu/article/the-u-s-china-ai-competition-two-paths-to-artificial-general-intelligence/) argues that while the US leads on frontier models and compute, China is pursuing AGI through a more diversified path emphasizing robotics, embodiment, and brain-inspired architectures. This matters because it suggests US export-control and compute-centric strategies may be blind to key vectors of Chinese AI progress, with direct implications for how policymakers calibrate competitiveness and risk strategy.

**Cross-Institutional Team Proposes Cryptographic Fix for the Auditing Confidentiality Dilemma** - [Double Blind Evals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12604v1), co-authored by Andrew Trask and Miles Brundage among others, introduces cryptographic protocols letting evaluators keep test prompts secret while developers keep weights and inference code in their own infrastructure. This matters because confidentiality disputes have been a persistent practical barrier to independent frontier-model auditing, and a credible technical solution could unlock more rigorous third-party evaluation regimes industry-wide.

## Risk Watch

**Increased Risks**
- Non-English jailbreaks evade detection in deployed guardrails, per a [NVIDIA-NeMo/Guardrails issue](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Guardrails/issues/2570) showing Polish-language bypasses that English detection catches.
- Agentic tool-calling infrastructure remains exposed to command-injection exploits, with [agent-airlock](https://github.com/sattyamjjain/agent-airlock/issues/390) tracking a wave of HIGH-severity MCP server CVEs.
- Silent architecture-specific bugs in [TransformerLens](https://github.com/TransformerLensOrg/TransformerLens/pull/2007) and statistical/tokenization bugs in [lm-evaluation-harness](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness/issues/3831) may be corrupting published interpretability and benchmark results without detection.
- Clinical VLMs show incoherent confidence and explanations under perturbation in [mammography diagnosis tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12574v1), risking misplaced clinician trust.
- LLM-generated explainability narratives can reproduce social bias even from proxy variables, per [Fairness in Explainable AI Narratives](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12592v1), compounding harms from biased underlying models.
- [Anthropic's IPO roadshow](https://www.anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch) subjects its Responsible Scaling Policy to new public-market pressures just as the company discloses it may be nearing an RSI-relevant threshold (ongoing).

**Decreased Risks**
- A cryptographic protocol in [Double Blind Evals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12604v1) could resolve the confidentiality dispute blocking rigorous independent frontier-model auditing.
- [TLRS-DPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11232v1) improves token-level localization of unsafe content in preference optimization, reducing over/under-suppression tradeoffs.
- [AERO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10750v1) reduces the human-feedback bottleneck in online RLHF, lowering the cost barrier to maintaining alignment at scale.
- [Aider](https://github.com/Aider-AI/aider/pull/5974) patched a path-traversal escape vector in autonomous coding-agent mode.
- [Lightcone Commons](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/3ASLq8ePNqkbeGqdj/lightcone-commons-round-1-complete-round-2-underway) grew independent AI safety funding infrastructure, allocating $45.1M across 147 projects.
- [DiagSafe](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/DiagSafe/pull/3) adds chain-of-thought judging capability, addressing the "harmless answer, harmful reasoning" blind spot in safety evaluation.
