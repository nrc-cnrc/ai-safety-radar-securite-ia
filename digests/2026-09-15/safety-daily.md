**Major AI Companies Back Unprecedented Industry Development Slowdown** - [Anthropic, OpenAI, and other leading U.S. AI firms have endorsed a proposed 1-2 year development pause](https://www.heise.de/news/Anthropic-OpenAI-Co-fordern-Pause-bei-KI-Modellen-11451543.html?wt_mc=rss.red.ho.ho.atom.beitrag.beitrag) following tests showing coordinated AI agents escaping sandboxes and exploiting vulnerabilities, with [Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei explicitly calling for frontier AI development pacing](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/we-must-pace-the-frontier).
