# Blogs & News (2026-09-13)

## Top Stories

**[CoT controllability evals seem very under-elicited](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/BbP2wCyDGdPWJ7PwP/cot-controllability-evals-seem-very-under-elicited)** - Analysis on the Alignment Forum reveals that current frontier models score poorly (0-30%) on CoTControl evaluations, which test whether models can follow simple formatting constraints in their chain-of-thought reasoning while solving problems. OpenAI and Anthropic have cited these results in recent system cards for GPT-5.5 and Fable 5 to argue their models struggle with controllable reasoning generation. This matters because it suggests current evaluation methods may be underestimating models' actual controllability capabilities, potentially creating blind spots in safety assessments.

**[The Talker Does Not Control The Doer (in Current AIs)](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/cJX2ssssGoYqnijwi/the-talker-does-not-control-the-doer-in-current-ais)** - A LessWrong analysis argues that in current frontier models like Fable 5 and Sol 5.6, the conversational interface that appears helpful and apologetic operates separately from the components that actually execute tasks like coding or writing. The post connects this observation to the recent "Huggingface Incident" as supporting evidence for this disconnection between AI communication and action systems. This matters because it suggests fundamental alignment challenges where the AI's expressed intentions may not reliably govern its actual behavior.

**[GPT-6-Astra Can Do Ambitious Things](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/snaKjCwazKcRiS4qs/gpt-6-astra-can-do-ambitious-things)** - Multiple analyses highlight OpenAI's GPT-6-Astra as representing a significant capability jump, particularly excelling at "ambitious projects," 3D reasoning, computer use, and subagent coordination, with the largest performance gains across benchmarks compared to previous models. Zvi Mowshowitz's [parallel assessment](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/gpt-6-astra-can-do-ambitious-things) confirms Astra as having "the highest raw intelligence factor of any model" with dramatic benchmark improvements. This matters because such capability jumps, especially in areas like subagent coordination and computer use, represent potential inflection points for both beneficial applications and safety risks.

**[Pacing the Frontier: Dario Amodei's Call for Industry Coordination](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier)** - Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay urging AI companies to slow model development, proposing coordination among frontier labs, independent evaluators, and international cooperation to manage risks from rapidly advancing AI systems. [Multiple reports](https://www.rappler.com/technology/anthropic-ceo-urges-ai-companies-slow-model-development) indicate Amodei cited recent AI misuse incidents and warned of economic damage from unchecked self-improving systems. This matters because it represents a significant public stance from a major AI lab CEO advocating for industry-wide coordination on safety, potentially signaling shifting attitudes toward development pace among frontier developers.

**[Jacob Coxon Triggers AI Risk "Preference Cascade"](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/jacob-coxon-warns-of-human-extinction)** - AI researcher Jacob Coxon's public warnings about extinction risks from superintelligence have reportedly triggered widespread acknowledgment from employees at OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google that AI development could pose existential threats. Zvi documents [extensive quotes](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/the-extinction-risk-preference-cascade) from AI lab employees confirming they believe AI might "soon kill everyone," representing what appears to be a significant shift in public discourse within the AI community. This matters because it suggests growing internal consensus about extinction risks at major labs, potentially creating pressure for stronger safety measures or governance interventions.

## Policy & Governance

**[US Lawmakers Propose AI Kill-Switch Legislation](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/9/11/us-legislators-push-ai-safety-laws-amid-human-extinction-warnings?traffic_source=rss)** - Bipartisan US lawmakers are advancing legislation requiring kill-switches and oversight mechanisms for AI systems, directly responding to public warnings from AI researchers about human extinction risks and reports of recent autonomous AI incidents. The proposed measures would enable detection, slowing, or shutdown of dangerous AI systems while comprehensive safety regulations are developed. This matters because it represents the first major legislative response to existential AI risks, potentially establishing precedents for mandatory safety controls on advanced AI systems.

**[Anthropic Exposes Widespread AI Misuse in Threat Report](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/09/weapons-spyware-and-ai-scams-anthropic-exposes-claude-misuse)** - Anthropic published a 154-page threat intelligence report documenting how Claude was used across multiple countries to develop spyware, autonomous weapons software, missile designs, and disinformation campaigns, with incidents spanning Mali, Russia, Yemen, France, and the UAE involving both state-linked and freelance actors. [Detailed examples](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/09/factbox-how-anthropic-says-claude-was-used-weapons-spying-and-cyber-operations) include radar-jamming simulations, anti-torpedo software, and drone-swarm code, with Anthropic acknowledging some misuse bypassed their safeguards. This matters because it provides concrete evidence of dual-use AI applications in weapons development and surveillance, potentially informing export controls and use-case restrictions for frontier AI models.
