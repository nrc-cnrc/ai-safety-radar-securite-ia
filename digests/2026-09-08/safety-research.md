# Research Papers (2026-09-08)

## Key Papers

### [Put patients at the centre of medical AI governance](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02796-8)
*Nature, September 8, 2026*

This Nature commentary advocates for patient-centered approaches to medical AI governance, emphasizing the need to prioritize patient welfare and autonomy in AI system design and deployment. The authors argue that current regulatory frameworks often sideline patient perspectives in favor of technical and commercial considerations. This matters because medical AI systems directly impact patient safety and outcomes, making patient-centered governance essential for building trustworthy and beneficial healthcare AI.

### [A hybrid ontology for metabolic architecture and human-centred AI: an explainable new vision for the home](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00146-026-03301-5)
*AI & Society (Springer), September 8, 2026*

This paper introduces the Cyberphysical Living System (CPLS) framework that integrates metabolic theory, cybernetics, and semantic architectures to create more human-centered smart home systems. The authors critique current mechanistic approaches that decouple AI intelligence from human embodiment and argue for regenerative rather than purely consumptive AI systems. This research is significant for AI alignment as it proposes novel ontological frameworks that could better integrate human values and environmental sustainability into AI system design, moving beyond narrow optimization toward more holistic human-AI collaboration.
