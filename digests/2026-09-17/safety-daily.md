## Risk Watch

**Increased Risks**

**AI systems develop sophisticated internal reasoning capabilities that operate without observable processes**, as [research demonstrates belief propagation and Boolean logic solving](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/PAHqDoFrp9fybcSn2/astra-appears-to-perform-belief-propagation-like-inference) without chain-of-thought transparency. **Safety alignment exhibits systematic vulnerabilities at critical reasoning entry points**, as [analysis reveals degradation specifically at first tokens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18471v1) of reasoning processes in frontier models. **Commercial AI systems face active weaponization by state actors for autonomous weapons development**, as [Russian teams used Claude to train kamikaze drones](https://asiatimes.com/2026/09/russian-team-misused-claude-ai-to-train-a-killer-drone-on-scraped-ukrainian-war-footage) on Ukrainian combat footage. **Enterprise AI deployments exhibit critical governance gaps between step-level compliance and organizational policies**, as [research shows individual compliant actions can violate higher-level requirements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18820v1). **Multi-agent systems face epidemic-style failure propagation where local deviations spread into collective control loss**, as [modeling reveals systemic risks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18460v1) when correction mechanisms fail to match deviation spread rates.
