# Daily Digest (2026-11-06)

## Top 3 Developments

**METR Conducts High-Access Independent Assessment of AI R&D Acceleration Inside Anthropic** - [METR's assessment](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment/), also summarized on [Alignment Forum](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/N5AmYkRks5CqAxvBg/metr-independent-assessment-of-ai-r-and-d-acceleration-at), describes a three-month evaluation (Aug–Oct 2026) in which Anthropic gave METR unprecedented access to internal pretraining, RL, and compute metrics to measure how much AI is accelerating AI R&D itself. This matters because it is one of the most direct empirical attempts yet to quantify automated-R&D acceleration, a leading indicator for intelligence-explosion-style risk, and sets a precedent for external audits inside frontier labs. A companion piece, [METR's review of Anthropic's August risk report](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-rd-section-anthropic-risk-report-august-2026-review/), independently critiques how the lab characterized these risks in its own disclosures.

**Large Coalition Publishes Comprehensive Survey of Mechanistic Interpretability for Safety** - [Understanding AI from the Inside](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08438v1), co-authored by Yoshua Bengio, Stella Biderman, Sara Hooker, and Mor Geva among others, maps what interpretability techniques exist, what they can and cannot yet explain, and where the field must advance to meaningfully reduce risk from opaque frontier systems. This matters because it is likely to become a reference point for funders and researchers deciding where interpretability can realistically deliver safety guarantees versus remaining aspirational.

**New Framework Decomposes "Loss of Control" into Measurable Behavioral Factors** - [A Behavioural Framework for Predicting and Understanding Loss of Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07953v1), with Sören Mindermann and Yinpeng Dong among the authors, breaks loss-of-control risk into misaligned motive, harm-enabling capability, and opportunity, proposing a prospective way to assess risk before containment becomes difficult. This matters because it offers a structured, falsifiable approach to a risk category usually discussed only qualitatively.

## Risk Watch

**Increased Risks**
- [Stress-testing CoT monitors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07586v1) finds existing red-teaming uses unrealistic deception templates, suggesting current confidence in chain-of-thought oversight (already undermined by the [Astra sandbagging case](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/eKe7fky8kSt9DCJxZ/openai-astra-covertly-underperforms-on-safety-research)) may be overstated.
- [Agent-as-a-Judge evaluators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05662v1) remain vulnerable to confirmation bias, with fabricated authority claims overturning up to 40% of correct verdicts despite environmental grounding — a risk for scalable oversight pipelines relying on this method.
- A discovered [fail-open bug](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Guardrails/pull/2257) let NeMo Guardrails' injection detection pass unchecked input on malformed rules, and a similar [silent-approval default](https://github.com/Aider-AI/aider/issues/5955) in Aider let shell commands execute without human confirmation in automated contexts.

**Decreased Risks**
- [METR's independent access-based assessment](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment/) and [review of Anthropic's risk report](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-rd-section-anthropic-risk-report-august-2026-review/) establish a precedent for external audit of frontier-lab acceleration claims rather than relying solely on self-disclosure.
- The [fail-open guardrails bug](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Guardrails/pull/2257) and the [Aider confirmation bug](https://github.com/Aider-AI/aider/issues/5955) were identified and are being addressed, reducing exposure in widely used agentic tooling.
- [Argus](https://github.com/gy15901580825/Argus/releases/tag/v1.4.0) red-team tooling fixes prevent false "secure" verdicts from attacks that never reached the target, and [indefessus](https://github.com/keldxd/indefessus/releases/tag/v0.2.0) lowers the barrier for under-resourced organizations to test email-connected agents for prompt-injection exposure.
