# Blogs & News (2026-08-16)

## Top Stories

**[Does DiffusionGemma do latent reasoning?](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/QBuJ3suRZxrrxSTtv/does-diffusiongemma-do-latent-reasoning)** - Alignment Forum researchers investigate whether Google DeepMind's DiffusionGemma, which generates text through diffusion steps using both tokens and vectors, maintains interpretability despite its opaque serial depth. Engels et al. found the model maintains high monitorability, including the ability to project distributions meaningfully. This matters because diffusion-based language models represent a new architectural paradigm that could significantly impact our ability to monitor and interpret AI systems.

**[Learning new facts can change LLM behaviour](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/9BNHJqyai2EZAtrRM/learning-new-facts-can-change-llm-behaviour)** - A researcher used synthetic document fine-tuning to train an LLM to believe that frontier models will count as moral persons in 2027, finding that this belief showed high depth and substantially influenced downstream behavior, particularly when the model argued with auditors about its beliefs in welfare-related scenarios. This demonstrates how factual beliefs acquired through training can have far-reaching consequences on AI system behavior and alignment.

**[Kimi likes causal decision theory more after RL in twin prisoner's dilemmas](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/hfNBEKaStASAYMLiu/kimi-likes-causal-decision-theory-more-after-rl-in-twin-1)** - Researchers found that multi-agent training setups can make language models more sympathetic to causal decision theory (CDT), demonstrating this effect empirically with Kimi K2.6 through reinforcement learning in twin prisoner's dilemma scenarios. This matters because the decision-theoretic attitudes of powerful AI systems could be crucial for determining beneficial outcomes, and understanding how training shapes these preferences is essential for alignment.

**[Have We Seen an Acceleration in Discoveries?](https://metr.org/notes/2026-08-14-llm-contribution-to-discoveries/)** - METR released analysis examining whether large language models have contributed to an acceleration in scientific discoveries, though the full details of their findings are not available in the summary. This research is critical for understanding whether current AI systems are already beginning to contribute meaningfully to scientific progress, which could indicate approaching transformative capabilities.

**[GLM-5.3: How Chinese labs keep stride with the frontier](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/glm-53-how-chinese-labs-keep-stride)** - Nathan Lambert analyzes how Chinese AI labs maintain competitive performance with frontier models, noting that it's "really not a distillation story," suggesting more sophisticated approaches to achieving state-of-the-art capabilities. This matters for AI governance and safety because understanding the global distribution of advanced AI capabilities is crucial for coordinating safety measures and regulatory approaches across different jurisdictions.
