# Blogs & News (2026-10-15)

## Top Stories

**METR Releases Comprehensive AI Risk Assessment Framework**
[METR](https://metr.org) published three significant posts outlining their approach to AI company collaboration and risk evaluation. Their [approach to working with AI companies](https://metr.org/blog/2026-10-14-our-approach-to-risk-assessment/) details methodologies for independent assessments, while companion pieces address [control and monitoring of internal AI agents](https://metr.org/blog/2026-10-14-control-assessment-proposal/) and [better public communication about loss-of-control risks](https://metr.org/blog/2026-10-14-better-informing-the-public-about-loss-of-control-risks/). This represents a major step toward standardized third-party evaluation capabilities that could inform both company practices and regulatory frameworks.

**DeepMind Chair Issues Stark Two-Year Warning on AI Incidents**
Google DeepMind chair Demis Hassabis [warned that increasingly capable AI systems could cause serious security incidents within two years](https://www.dawn.com/news/2037119/ai-could-cause-major-incidents-within-2-years-without-safeguards-says-google-deepmind-chair) without stronger safeguards, urging companies to test new models in offline settings. This unusually direct timeline from a leading AI executive signals growing concern about the pace of capability advancement relative to safety measures.

**400 Researchers Call for International AI Safety Body**
[About 400 researchers from 34 countries, including AI pioneers Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio, are calling for an international body to oversee advanced AI safety](https://www.rappler.com/technology/scientists-call-international-body-oversee-advanced-ai), proposing shared containment rules and public incident reporting. The scale and prominence of signatories suggests growing scientific consensus that voluntary industry commitments are insufficient for managing frontier AI risks.

**AI Constitutional AI Research Shows Value Drift Dynamics**
A new [Alignment Forum post explores "continual constitutional conventions"](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/c9woewdY9ddsPqf3J/the-continual-constitutional-convention-how-language-models) where language models iteratively revise their own governing documents, simulating value reflection processes. This research provides crucial insights into how AI systems might autonomously evolve their ethical frameworks over time, with significant implications for long-term alignment.

**Zvi Critiques Lab Safety Cultures**
AI safety commentator Zvi Mowshowitz published a scathing assessment arguing that [safety culture at frontier labs is fundamentally broken](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/the-safety-culture-at-the-labs-is), pointing to systemic issues beyond individual failures. This aligns with growing external pressure on companies to demonstrate genuine safety commitments rather than public relations efforts.

## Policy & Governance

**Anthropic Strengthens Election Safeguards for 2026 Midterms**
[Anthropic detailed enhanced measures for the 2026 US midterms](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-us-midterms), building on earlier election safeguards to prevent political bias, detect misuse, and direct users to authoritative voting information. This represents continued industry adaptation to concerns about AI's role in democratic processes and information integrity.

**AI Safety Newsletter Covers White House Industry Accord**
The [CAIS AI Safety Newsletter reports that six AI companies signed a new White House accord](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/aisn-83-six-ai-companies-sign-white), though details of commitments remain limited. The ongoing pattern of voluntary industry agreements continues amid calls for more binding regulatory frameworks.
