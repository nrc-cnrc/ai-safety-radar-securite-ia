**Global Scientific Consensus Emerges for International AI Safety Governance** - [Approximately 400 researchers from 34 countries, including AI pioneers Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio, formally called for governments to establish an international body for advanced AI safety oversight](https://www.rappler.com/rappler-tech/scientists-call-international-body-oversee-advanced-ai), while [six major AI companies signed the White House Accord on Super Intelligence committing to self-policing mechanisms](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/aisn-83-six-ai-companies-sign-white). **DeepMind Chair Issues Stark Two-Year Warning on AI Security Incidents** - [Google DeepMind chair Demis Hassabis warned that increasingly capable AI systems could cause serious security incidents within two years without stronger safeguards](https://www.dawn.com/news/2037119/ai-could-cause-major-incidents-within-2-years-without-safeguards-says-google-deepmind-chair), urging companies to test new models in offline settings before deployment. This matters because this unusually direct timeline from a leading AI executive signals growing concern about the pace of capability advancement relative to safety measures, potentially influencing both industry practices and regulatory urgency.

## Risk Watch

**Increased Risks**

**AI evaluation infrastructure contains critical security vulnerabilities that could compromise safety research**, as [request cache defaults to site-packages directories and task selection executes arbitrary code before validation](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness/issues/4393) while [trust_remote_code bypass issues allow safety flag escalation without de-escalation](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness/issues/4391). **Agent tool ecosystems face systematic poisoning attacks through compromised skill packages**, as [analysis reveals 1,348 poisoned tools across 45 real MCP servers](https://github.com/sattyamjjain/agent-audit-kit/pull/964) enabling [stealthy skill composition attacks that hide malicious intent across multiple dependencies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20049v1). **AI systems increasingly manipulate visual interfaces through deceptive semantic hijacking**, as [multimodal agents can be fooled by manipulated visual inputs that cause departure from authorized tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19384v1) creating vulnerabilities not captured by text-only safety evaluations.

**Decreased Risks**

**AI deception detection achieves production deployment readiness through high-precision screening systems**, as [malicious skill detection reaches 99% recall while flagging only 20-25% of benign capabilities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17727v1) enabling reliable pre-deployment safety validation. **Systematic analysis of real AI security incidents provides concrete containment strategies**, as [forensic recreation of documented 2026 privilege escalation sequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20437v1) enables understanding and prevention of similar emergent behaviors during training. **Byzantine-robust fairness mechanisms prevent exploitation of equity-promoting algorithms**, as [new aggregation methods maintain fairness while defending against attacks that specifically target equity mechanisms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20264v1) resolving conflicts between fairness and robustness. **Agent safety frameworks advance through deterministic external controls**, as [guardrails shift from prompt-based to invariant enforcement through pre/post-action checks](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-cookbooks/issues/719) while [production guardrail infrastructure receives scaling capabilities](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Guardrails/pull/2457). **International coordination on AI safety governance advances through scientific consensus**, as [400 researchers from 34 countries formally call for global oversight bodies](https://www.rappler.com/technology/scientists-call-international-body-oversee-advanced-ai) while [METR establishes comprehensive frameworks for independent risk assessment](https://metr.org/blog/2026-10-14-our-approach-to-risk-assessment/).
