# Blogs & News (2026-07-30)

## Top Stories

**[Frontier Lab Employee Open Letter Calls For Being Able to Pace the Frontier](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/frontier-lab-employee-open-letter)** - Zvi Mowshowitz reports on what he calls "the most important open letter in years," where over 1,100 employees from major tech firms including Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, and Google are [urging the U.S. to back an international effort called "Pacing the Frontier"](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/tech-employees-call-us-backed-global-effort-manage-risks-advanced-ai-6284401) to develop technical and governance tools to slow advanced AI development. This represents unprecedented internal industry pressure for external regulation and coordination on AI development timelines.

**[Anthropic's AI finds flaw halving NIST candidate key strength](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/07/mythos-uncovers-crypto-weaknesses-that-went-unknown-for-years)** - Anthropic's Mythos security model discovered critical weaknesses in HAWK, a NIST post-quantum signature candidate, and an AES instance, prompting HAWK's developer to withdraw the candidate entirely. This demonstrates how advanced AI systems are beginning to make novel discoveries in cybersecurity that could have significant implications for cryptographic standards.

**[Claude Opus 5 Is Highly Capable, But Is No Mythos](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/claude-opus-5-is-highly-capable-but)** - Zvi Mowshowitz analyzes Anthropic's latest Claude Opus 5 release, noting it as "a weirder than usual release to evaluate" and distinguishing its capabilities from the specialized Mythos security model. This suggests Anthropic is developing increasingly specialized AI systems for different domains rather than pursuing purely general capability increases.

**[Value Generalisation 3: Pre-aligned AIs](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/uMKGaEKRDpoqnZyBh/value-generalisation-3-pre-aligned-ais)** - A research post exploring the concept of "pre-aligned generalising AIs" that could potentially resolve the traditional tension between alignment and capabilities by building value alignment into the fundamental generalization mechanisms. This represents an important theoretical contribution to solving alignment challenges before they become intractable at higher capability levels.

**[Visual prompt engineering for video models](https://deepmind.google/research/publications/264392/)** - Google DeepMind published research on visual prompt engineering techniques for video foundation models, extending the concept of prompt engineering from language to visual domains. This highlights the continued expansion of foundation model paradigms across modalities, with implications for how we understand and control these systems' behavior.
